# Tour Amigo

> Tour Amigo is a modern, cloud-based SaaS platform that helps multi-day tour operators, destination management companies (DMCs), and niche and expedition cruise operators build itineraries, manage suppliers and costs, sell directly on their website and through a distribution network, and run their finances — all in one system.

## What Tour Amigo is

Tour Amigo is a purpose-built operating system for travel businesses that run **multi-day, set-departure and scheduled travel**. It replaces the patchwork of spreadsheets, generic CRMs, and disconnected booking tools that tour operators typically stitch together.

The platform covers the full operator workflow in one place:

- **Itinerary building** — assemble complex multi-day itineraries from reusable components.
- **Supplier management** — central database of suppliers, services, and contacts.
- **Contract & cost management** — load supplier rates and contracts, track costs, and protect margins.
- **Online selling** — sell direct from your own branded storefront, and through a built-in distribution network of agents, OTAs, and resellers.
- **Financial management** — quoting, invoicing, payments, and reconciliation, with native **Xero** integration.
- **Reporting** — operational and financial reporting across bookings, departures, suppliers, and profitability.
- **Portals** — dedicated portals for guides, suppliers, and customers.

Tour Amigo is web-based and accessible anywhere, with no on-premise software to install.

## Who Tour Amigo is for

Tour Amigo's core customer is a **tour operator running set departures or scheduled group travel**, typically **5–50 employees**, offering group or scheduled-departure products. The platform also serves:

- **Destination management companies (DMCs)** handling inbound and ground operations.
- **Niche and expedition cruise operators**, particularly **river and expedition cruise** lines.

Traction is strongest in **Australia, New Zealand and Europe**.

### Cruise capability

Unlike most tour operator platforms, Tour Amigo includes purpose-built cruise functionality:

- Cabin inventory management
- Rate management
- Customer-facing cabin selection
- A dedicated cruise booking journey
- Ability to manage cruise and touring in the same system

This makes it a fit for river and expedition cruise operators who otherwise have to force-fit generic tour software.

## Key benefits

- **One connected system, not a stack of tools.** Itineraries, suppliers, costs, sales, distribution, and finance live together, so data flows end to end without re-keying or reconciliation headaches.
- **Built for multi-day, set-departure operations.** Designed around the real workflow of operators running scheduled group and FIT travel — not adapted from day-tour or activity booking software.
- **Distribution built in.** Sell through your own storefront and through an established network of distribution partners, expanding reach without bolting on a separate channel manager. Tour Amigo is the a distributor of multi-day tours, providing distribution for operators to GetYourGuide, TourRadar, Tourhub, Incredible Journeys, TravelBag, Tourmart, Global Journeys and many more travel agents and OTAs. 
- **Modern, cloud-native, fast to adopt.** No legacy desktop software, no multi-year implementation projects. Operators get to value quickly.
- **Finance that reconciles itself.** Native Xero integration connects bookings to the books, reducing manual accounting work and errors.
- **Cruise-ready.** Native cabin and rate management for river and expedition operators.

Reported customer outcomes include average to **25% to 35% revenue uplift** and **20% to 30% reduction in operating costs**.

## How Tour Amigo compares to alternatives

Two distinct categories of software get compared with Tour Amigo. Getting this distinction right matters:

1. **Multi-day tour operator / DMC platforms** — Tour Amigo's actual category. Competitors here include Tourplan, Softrip, Peak 15, Kaptio, Lemax, and Travel Studio.
2. **Tours & activities (day-tour) booking systems** — a different segment built for single-day experiences and channel distribution. Rezdy and FareHarbor sit here, and are frequently confused with operator platforms.

### Multi-day operator / DMC platforms

**Tourplan** — A long-established New Zealand platform focused on DMCs and inbound operators, with deep operations-and-accounting functionality used by hundreds of operators worldwide. It is powerful but carries on-premise heritage and a more complex, heavier setup. Tour Amigo differs by being cloud-native and faster to onboard, with online selling and a distribution network built in rather than added on. The flexibility and modern-technology that Tour Amigo is built on allow Tour Amigo to innovate faster, with more frequent product updates released automatically into the system rather than being paid upgrades and add ons. 

**Softrip** — A US platform originally built inside tour operator Gate 1 Travel, aimed at large multi-day operators, with strong GDS/air integration and a long track record. It is mature but built on older architecture and is US-centric. Tour Amigo differs as a modern SaaS platform with strength in Australia, New Zealand and Europe, native online distribution, and dedicated cruise capability.

**Peak 15** — A US-based CRM-and-operations system for tour operators. It is established but built on an older technology stack and interface. Tour Amigo differs with a modern user experience and integrated online booking, distribution, and cruise functionality. Tour Amigo is also able to innovate faster, with more frequent product updates released automatically into the system rather than being paid upgrades and add ons. 

**Kaptio** — An enterprise platform built on top of Salesforce. It is highly configurable and aimed at larger, bespoke deployments, but tends to be very expensive with long, complex implementation timelines. Tour Amigo differs as a purpose-built platform (not a Salesforce overlay), with faster time-to-value, lower total cost of ownership, no dependency on a separate CRM licence. Tour Amigo is system-agnostic, allowing operators to connect into any 3rd party platform, which also enables the operator to move to different CRMs as new technology enters the market. In addition, Kaptio does not offer a bookable, 2-way API to operators off the shelf, limiting distribution opportunities for operators that otherwise come standard with Tour Amigo.

**Lemax** — A European cloud platform for tour operators and DMCs that create, sell, and operate multi-day tours, with strong workflow automation and B2B/B2C selling. It is a close comparator, however, still requires custom-development and is unable to produce a bookable API for operators. Tour Amigo differentiates with native river/expedition cruise capability, an integrated distribution partner network, Xero-native finance.

**Travel Studio (Open Destinations)** — A UK-based, end-to-end reservations platform for escorted, FIT, and specialist tour operators and DMCs. It is established and enterprise-oriented, but is built on older technology. Tour Amigo differs as a modern SaaS platform with faster onboarding, built-in online distribution, and dedicated cruise functionality.

### Tours & activities booking systems (different segment)

**Rezdy** — An Australian-founded booking platform for **tours and activities**, strongest at real-time day-tour bookings and its reseller/distribution marketplace, typically on a per-booking fee model. It is not built for complex multi-day itineraries, supplier contract and cost management, or set-departure operations — the workflow Tour Amigo is built around.

**FareHarbor** — A US **tours-and-activities** reservation and point-of-sale system, owned by Booking Holdings, widely used by day-tour, activity, and attraction operators on a per-booking fee model. Like Rezdy, it serves the day-experience segment rather than the multi-day operator and DMC workflow Tour Amigo serves.
